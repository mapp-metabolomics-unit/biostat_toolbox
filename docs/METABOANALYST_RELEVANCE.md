# MetaboAnalyst 6.0: relevance to Biostats batch 00280

## Scope and evidence

This is a **method-compatibility audit**, not a claim of numerical equivalence with MetaboAnalyst or a proposal to call its web service. MetaboAnalyst 6.0's [home/module overview](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/MetaboAnalyst/home.xhtml) advertises statistical, metadata, biomarker, functional, raw-spectral, dose-response and causal modules. The [official tutorials index](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/Tutorials.xhtml) warns that older screenshots may not match the current interface; the [statistical vignette](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/resources/vignettes/Statistical_Analysis_Module.html), [preprocessing vignette](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/resources/vignettes/Introductions.html), [batch-correction tutorial](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/RTutorial.xhtml), [metadata-analysis tutorial](https://api2.xialab.ca/api/download/metaboanalyst/7_Statistical_analysis_metadata.pdf) and [data formats](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/Format.xhtml) distinguish the method and input requirements below.

Inputs are an MZmine peak-*height* table and treated metadata, with optional SIRIUS and horizontal **candidate** annotation tables ([batch dataset](../../laure-weisskopf-group/docs/mapp_project_00016/mapp_batch_00280/recipes/dataset.yaml); [treated metadata](../../laure-weisskopf-group/docs/mapp_project_00016/mapp_batch_00280/metadata/treated/mapp_batch_00280_metadata.tsv)). The **historical, now superseded** analysis retained 4,167 features from 345 biological samples, 40 pooled QCs and 9 blanks; 142 of those lacked any observed positive biological signal and 1,980 appeared in fewer than 20% of biological injections. `injection_order` exists with QCs spanning positions 4–391 (maximum observed QC gap 13; nearest-QC distance for a biological injection at most six). The former implementation used a **single matrix-wide half-minimum** and imputed absent QCs before RSD filtering. The revised [`preprocess.R`](../src/biostats/preprocess.R) uses per-feature biological half-minima and observed-positive QC support; the 30% QC-RSD and 5:1 blank settings are still batch choices, **not** drift correction or equivalents of the different 20% example in the [MetaboAnalystR filtering vignette](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/resources/vignettes/Introductions.html#filtering-an-example-dataset-compound-concentration-data).

Already available: study PCA scores/loadings/variance and PCoA; comparison feature-wise contrasts and omnibus F-tests with BH q-values; batch/time/group linear models, paired blocking where verified, Hellinger PERMANOVA **and** dispersion, and guarded fold-local PLS-DA with validation/permutations. The dashboard has score plots, feature intensity boxplots, volcano plots, candidate-annotation exploration, and downloadable plotted data ([analysis](../src/biostats/analysis.R), [model](../src/biostats/linear_model.R), [variance](../src/biostats/variance.R), [PLS-DA](../src/biostats/supervised.R), [dashboard guide](STATS_PIPELINE_BIOSTATS.md)). Newly added are observed-detection/QC-drift diagnostic tables, an **optional, disabled-by-default** QC-only LOESS correction, an omnibus viewer and a bounded descriptive hierarchical feature heatmap. These are **not** feature correlation/pattern tests, empirical-Bayes differential tests, ASCA/MEBA, OPLS-DA, random forests or ROC biomarker models merely because they share inputs or a graph. Analyses must save configured parameters and results in the checksum-sealed run rather than invent inferential results in the browser.

## Ranked reusable additions

| Rank | Addition and why now | Validated input → output / study design | Main limitations and implementation needs | Official method reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Observed detection, QC support, and feature-specific missing-value handling.** The historical 142 never-observed retained features exposed a concrete defect before any classifier could be trusted. | `feature_detection.tsv` records observed-positive biological counts and exclusion of zero-supported features; `blank_filter.tsv` and `qc_rsd.tsv` record measured blank and QC support. Per-feature biological half-minima replace missing/nonpositive retained values; no imputed QC can make a feature pass the ≥80%-supported QC RSD filter. | A universal 20%/50% biological prevalence cutoff can erase treatment-exclusive signals, so no such cutoff was added. The output does not stratify missingness by group/time or prove a detection mechanism; inspect sensitivity before changing the filter. MetaboAnalystR's examples include alternative imputation/filtering, not a basis for declaring methods equivalent. | [MetaboAnalystR processing: `RemoveMissingByPercent`, `ImputeMissingVar`, `ReplaceMin`, filtering](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/resources/vignettes/Introductions.html) |
| **2** | **Measured QC stability and guarded optional injection-order correction.** QC RSD alone cannot show acquisition-order association. | `qc_drift.tsv` records observed-positive QC counts, missingness, before/after RSD, order Spearman trend, fit/coverage and correction status. Optional QC-only LOESS is **disabled in this batch**, needs adequate support and QC coverage, bounds correction, and does not extrapolate beyond QC support. | Technical runs/QC material and order-by-batch confounding still require scientific inspection; correlations do not prove technical drift. No per-run QC-RLSC, local QC trajectory graph or calibration to biological controls is claimed. Correction would require a different study and sensitivity comparisons. | [MetaboAnalystR batch effect and signal drift tutorial, §3.1–3.1.2](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/RTutorial.xhtml) |
| **3** | **Bounded feature heatmap and hierarchical clustering.** A descriptive view of a selected subset complements PCA and feature boxplots. | The browser uses the saved checksum-verified scaled biological matrix with group/time/batch annotations; selects a bounded number of unsupervised high-variance features or an explicit feature list, clusters sample and feature axes using Euclidean distance/average linkage, and exports the plotted values. | Display ordering is exploratory, not a new sealed inference; scaled values are not raw abundances. No inferential feature-correlation/pattern search was added: repeated plants and selected strata would need explicit sampling/design and multiplicity rules. | [MetaboAnalystR statistical vignette: clustering and heatmaps](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/resources/vignettes/Statistical_Analysis_Module.html); [metadata tutorial, pp. 10–14](https://api2.xialab.ca/api/download/metaboanalyst/7_Statistical_analysis_metadata.pdf) |
| **4 · deferred** | **Moderated feature-wise inference for prespecified contrasts.** Shrinking residual variances can help when there are many measured features and small strata, but is an alternative estimator, not a harmless display option. | Would require a validated full-rank biological-only design with explicit contrast orientation, unit/block handling and a separate effect/statistic/p/BH-q output next to the current ordinary tests; the environment lists `limma` ([lockfile](../biostats/renv.lock)). | Small severity cells, repeated plants, batch aliasing and residual degrees of freedom require design review and sensitivity checks first. Do **not** relabel a potential limma empirical-Bayes test as MetaboAnalyst **EBAM**, a different posterior-mixture method, or claim that current ordinary F tests are EBAM. | [MetaboAnalystR statistical vignette: EBAM versus ordinary tests](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/resources/vignettes/Statistical_Analysis_Module.html) |

**Order of work:** first make the existing missing-data/retention behavior visible; diagnose technical drift rather than correct by default; then add bounded exploratory visualization; only then evaluate a new inferential estimator. Each row requires both a pipeline result/table and a display/interpretation contract, provenance, and named independent or paired design. The 345 biological rows do **not** constitute 345 independent units for every test: the [complete-plant recipe](../../laure-weisskopf-group/docs/mapp_project_00016/mapp_batch_00280/recipes/study_complete_plants.yaml) describes 112 plants observed at T0, T1 and T3, and [paired recipes](../../laure-weisskopf-group/docs/mapp_project_00016/mapp_batch_00280/recipes/paired_S49_D.yaml) explicitly block by `ATTRIBUTE_plant_uid`. Current [interaction recipes](../../laure-weisskopf-group/docs/mapp_project_00016/mapp_batch_00280/recipes/model_interaction_T1.yaml) already fit group × batch terms; an ordinary two-way ANOVA label alone is not new functionality.

**Implemented from the shortlist:** Rank 1's observed biological/QC feature support and per-feature half-minimum (not a universal missingness filter, groupwise detection table or automated sensitivity study); rank 2's observed-QC RSD/order summary and guarded optional LOESS correction **disabled in this batch** (not per-run QC-RLSC); rank 3's bounded descriptive, browser-side heatmap of an existing checksum-verified scaled matrix (not a new hypothesis test or correlation estimator). The heatmap computes its display ordering in-browser; its source matrices and preprocessing belong to the sealed study. Rank 4's alternative moderated estimator is **not implemented**: its effect on small, partly confounded repeated-plant strata requires separate design review, and MetaboAnalyst's EBAM cannot honestly be relabeled as limma. These distinctions limit what “MetaboAnalyst-like” means for this dataset.

## Not applicable now, or conditional rather than a priority

- **Reprocessing raw LC-MS spectra:** MetaboAnalyst 6.0 and MetaboAnalystR support centroided raw LC-MS/MS feature detection/alignment and annotation, and MetaboAnalystR explicitly interoperates with **MZmine** peak tables/MSP files ([home](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/MetaboAnalyst/home.xhtml), [R tutorial §3.3](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/RTutorial.xhtml), [formats](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/Format.xhtml)). This study's input is *already* MZmine quantification; replacing peak picking is a separate upstream scientific analysis, not a missing statistical method.
- **Compound-set enrichment, pathway topology and knowledge-network inference:** these need defensible compound identities/mappings and appropriate organism/pathway coverage, not unvalidated one-candidate-per-feature conversion. SIRIUS/Canopus/horizontal tables here are *candidate* evidence; identical 2D keys may represent ambiguous structures. The [home page](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/MetaboAnalyst/home.xhtml) distinguishes named-metabolite enrichment/pathway methods from *mummichog/GSEA MS peaks to pathways*. The latter can accept m/z, optional retention time, p and t statistics **without confirmed identity** ([official peak input specifications](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/Format.xhtml), [R functional-analysis tutorial](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/RTutorial.xhtml)); it is a possible **separate, hypothesis-generating** analysis once valid mass/RT, ion polarity, organism/network and independent contrast statistics are vetted, **not** proof of identified compound pathways. Joint pathway analysis additionally needs aligned gene data ([R tutorial §2.8](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/RTutorial.xhtml)).
- **Clinical biomarker ROC/random forest/SVM/elastic-net classifiers:** the source modules are for prediction and held-out evaluation, with ROC defined for two groups ([home](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/MetaboAnalyst/home.xhtml), [formats](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/Format.xhtml)). This is a repeated-plant infection/treatment study, not a patient diagnostic cohort; severity comparisons select tiny low/high strata ([batch severity comparison](../../laure-weisskopf-group/docs/mapp_project_00016/mapp_batch_00280/recipes/disease_control_B1_T0.yaml)). Claiming reliable clinical sensitivity/specificity or external validation from these cells would be unjustified; existing guarded PLS-DA is **not** an RF/SVM/ROC pipeline.
- **Dose response and causal mGWAS:** MetaboAnalyst's dose-response module models measured exposure/doses for benchmark-dose estimation, while mGWAS/Mendelian randomization needs genetic association/instrument and outcome summary statistics ([home](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/MetaboAnalyst/home.xhtml), [6.0 tutorial index](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/Tutorials.xhtml)). No dose series or GWAS instruments exist in this dataset; T0/T1/T3 are sampling **times**, not dose levels.
- **Cross-study biomarker/functional meta-analysis:** requires comparable independent studies and matched feature identities/conditions, not treating three batches or repeated plants as three studies ([home](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/MetaboAnalyst/home.xhtml), [formats](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/Format.xhtml)).
- **ASCA/MEBA time series and power analysis:** **conditional/deferred, not intrinsically impossible**. MetaboAnalyst advertises ASCA and MEBA for two-factor/time-series designs; official time-series format asks for balanced subjects and named time/subject columns ([home](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/MetaboAnalyst/home.xhtml), [metadata tutorial pp. 15–17](https://api2.xialab.ca/api/download/metaboanalyst/7_Statistical_analysis_metadata.pdf), [formats](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/Format.xhtml)). The 112 complete plants offer an explicit repeated-unit subset, but all 345 samples cannot be treated as balanced independent repeated measures, and existing per-feature interaction tests are **not** ASCA/MEBA. Power analysis needs an explicit target effect, endpoint, error/FDR assumptions and design; it cannot be inferred from PCA or sample count alone ([R tutorial §2.11](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/docs/RTutorial.xhtml)).
